About Break Free

Break Free was created to help teenagers and young adults develop healthier relationships with their phones.

Instead of simply asking users to stop using their phones, Break Free provides short activities that encourage users to move, think, explore, and interact with their surroundings.

Our Activities

Math Challenges

Solve a quick math problem to give your mind something different to focus on.

Mini Games

Complete a short game designed to provide a quick alternative to scrolling.

Timed Breaks

Step away from your phone for a set amount of time and focus on your surroundings.

Photo Challenges

Explore your surroundings and complete a photo challenge to encourage creativity and awareness.

Our Goal

The goal of Break Free is to make taking a break from your phone feel simple, engaging, and achievable. By completing small activities, users can build a habit of taking intentional breaks throughout their day.
