Break Free helps you step away from your phone by giving you short, engaging activities that encourage movement, creativity, and interaction with the world around you.

How Break Free Works

1. Start a Break

Tap the Start Break button when you are ready to take intentional time away from your phone.

2. Choose an Activity

Pick from short challenges such as math problems, mini games, timed breaks, and photo challenges.

3. Complete the Challenge

Complete your activity and spend a few moments interacting with your surroundings.

4. Track Your Progress

View your completed challenges and see your progress through your Activity History.

Why Break Free?

Break Free is designed for teenagers and young adults who want to develop healthier phone-use habits. Instead of simply telling users to put their phones down, Break Free gives them something positive to do during their break.
