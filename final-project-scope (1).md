# Break Free: A Wellness App

## Project Purpose

The app helps users reduce phone use by requiring them to complete short activities before returning to normal phone use. These activities encourage users to get out of bed, move around, and interact with their surroundings.

### Project Objectives

- Encourage users to take intentional breaks from their phones
- Provide short activities including math problems, games, timed breaks, and photo challenges
- Track completed challenges and user progress
- Provide a simple experience for teenagers and young adults

### Product Scope

The app will include:

**Home Screen** with progress and a "Start Break" button
**Choose Activity Screen** for selecting a challenge
**Challenge Screen** for completing the activity
**Activity History** for viewing completed challenges

### Major Deliverables

- User flow and interface designs
- Functional mobile app
- Challenge activities
- Progress tracking
- Activity History

### In Scope

- Mobile app design and development
- Brain break functionality
- Progress and activity tracking
- Basic testing and usability evaluation

### Out of Scope

- Medical or mental health treatment
- Advanced behavioral analysis
- Complete control over all phone applications

### Constraints and Assumptions

The project limited by development time, technical restrictions on controlling phone use, and access to device features such as the camera. 

### Acceptance Criteria

The project can be successful when users can start a break, choose and complete a challenge, have their progress recorded, and view their completed activities.

### Milestones

1. Define requirements and scope
2. Design the user flow
3. Develop the app
4. Test and improve the app.
